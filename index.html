NEW TO MARIO? START HERE controls · power-ups · warp zones

🎮 CONTROLS (NES)

  • ← → Walk
  • A Jump. Hold it to jump higher.
  • B Hold to run. Tap to throw fireballs.
  • ↓ Crouch (when big) or enter a pipe
  • START Pause

🍄 POWER-UPS

  • Super Mushroom: grow big and survive one extra hit.
  • Fire Flower: throw fireballs with B.
  • Starman: a few seconds of invincibility.
  • 1-Up Mushroom (green): an extra life.
  • 100 coins: also an extra life.

🗺 HOW THE GAME IS LAID OUT

There are 8 worlds with 4 levels each. Levels 1–3 end at a flagpole. Level 4 is always a castle where you face Bowser.

The first seven Bowsers are fakes (other enemies in disguise). Hit one with fireballs to see what it really is.

🌀 WARP ZONES (SHORTCUTS)

  • 1-2: run along the ceiling at the end → Worlds 2, 3, 4
  • 4-2: climb a hidden vine → Worlds 6, 7, 8
  • 4-2: run along the ceiling at the end → World 5

Skipping is fun, but playing every level is the best way to learn.

🃏 READING A LEVEL CARD

LEVEL 1-2 means World 1, level 2. The ■■■□□ meter shows difficulty from 1 to 5.
